KSP DeltaV Planner

An interactive Delta-V map and CommNet relay calculator for Kerbal Space Program.

Built-in maps cover Stock, Outer Planets Mod (OPM), Real Solar System (RSS), and Kcalbeloh with Stock or OPM.

Delta-V mission planning

Choose an origin and destination, add intermediate stops, and compare one-way, round-trip, or return-only missions. Add a reserve and inspect maneuver estimates. A Stock Kerbin surface to Mun surface journey has a reference budget of 5,120 m/s before reserves.

Relays and signal strength

Compare antenna setups, plan relay coverage around a body's equator, and estimate the burns to deploy the relays. Check one link between craft near planets using simplified circular orbits and a check for blocking by their central body. Kerbalism presets also estimate science transmission rates.

Figures are planning estimates. The tool does not calculate positions from a save date or a complete chain of relays back to the ground network.
